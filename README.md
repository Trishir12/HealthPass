# HealthPass

HealthPass is a patient-centric digital health record platform designed to make medical history secure, portable, and easily accessible with patient consent.

## Vision

Healthcare records in many clinics and hospitals are still fragmented across paper files or isolated digital systems. Patients often need to carry physical records, repeat their medical history, or undergo duplicate tests when visiting a new healthcare provider.

HealthPass aims to provide patients with a single, secure identity for accessing and sharing their medical history while making it easier for healthcare providers to retrieve relevant patient information.

## Problem Statement

Patients face several challenges with traditional medical record management:

- Physical medical files can be lost, damaged, or forgotten.
- Medical history is scattered across multiple hospitals and clinics.
- Doctors often lack access to previous diagnoses and treatments.
- Repeated tests increase both cost and time.
- There is no simple, patient-controlled way to share records across healthcare providers.

## Proposed Solution

HealthPass provides:

- A secure patient health profile
- Digital medical history management
- QR-based HealthPass for quick patient identification
- Consent-based sharing of records between participating clinics
- A lightweight web platform for clinics and patients

> Note: HealthPass is currently in the research and MVP design phase.

## MVP Goals
